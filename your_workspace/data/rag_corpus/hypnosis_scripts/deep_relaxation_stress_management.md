## Script Overview

This script induces deep relaxation and teaches the mind-body system to release stress and return to a calm baseline state. Particularly effective for chronic stress, tension, and overstimulation.

---

## Pre-Induction (2 min)

You've come here today to give yourself the gift of deep relaxation... to release the accumulated stress and tension... and to return to your natural state of calm...

Your body knows how to relax... Your nervous system remembers peace... Today, you're simply reminding it... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Give yourself full permission now... permission to let go... permission to rest... permission to simply be...

---

## Induction (5 min)

Begin with a slow, deep breath... filling your lungs completely... *[pause 2 seconds]*... and releasing with a sigh of relief... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Again... breathing in calm... *[pause 2 seconds]*... breathing out stress... *[pause 3 seconds]*

And one more time... letting each exhale carry away a little more tension... *[pause 4 seconds]*

Now allow your breath to find its own natural rhythm... You don't need to control it... just notice it... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Bring your awareness to your feet... Notice any sensations there... warmth... coolness... pressure... *[pause 2 seconds]*

And imagine that with each exhale, your feet become a little heavier... a little warmer... completely relaxed... *[pause 3 seconds]*

This wave of relaxation moves up slowly... into your ankles... your calves... like warm honey flowing through your legs... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Through your knees... your thighs... *[pause 2 seconds]*... releasing... softening... letting go... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Into your hips... your lower back... *[pause 2 seconds]*... any tightness there can melt away now... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Your abdomen softens... rising and falling with each gentle breath... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Your chest expands effortlessly... your heart beats steadily... calmly... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Your shoulders drop... perhaps an inch... or two... releasing all that weight... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Down through your arms... your elbows... your forearms... *[pause 2 seconds]*... into your hands... which rest completely... palms open... nothing to hold... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Your neck releases... your jaw unclenches... your tongue rests softly... *[pause 3 seconds]*

All the tiny muscles around your eyes relax... your forehead smooths... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Your whole scalp releases... and your entire body is now in this beautiful state of deep relaxation...

---

## Deepening (3 min)

In this state of comfort and ease, I invite you to imagine a peaceful place... perhaps a beach... a forest... a mountain meadow... or anywhere that feels safe and calm to you... *[pause 3 seconds]*

See the details of this place... the colors... the light... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Hear the sounds... perhaps gentle waves... or wind through trees... or the quiet of stillness... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Feel the temperature... comfortable... perfect... *[pause 2 seconds]*

And notice how being in this place allows you to relax even more deeply... *[pause 3 seconds]*

With each breath in this peaceful place, you drift deeper... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Deeper into calm... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Deeper into peace... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Deeper into complete relaxation... *[pause 4 seconds]*

---

## Core Work (6 min)

### Body Scan and Release

I'd like you to scan through your body now... and notice if there are any areas still holding tension... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Perhaps in your jaw... or shoulders... or lower back... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Just notice... without judgment... *[pause 3 seconds]*

And breathe into that area... *[pause 2 seconds]*... imagining that your breath brings warm, healing energy... *[pause 3 seconds]*... and carries away the tension as you exhale... *[pause 4 seconds]*

### Stress Release Imagery

Imagine now that all the stress you've been carrying has a color... or a texture... or a weight... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Just notice what stress looks like to you... *[pause 3 seconds]*

And with each exhale... imagine this stress leaving your body... like clouds dissolving... or water flowing away... *[pause 3 seconds]*

With each breath, less and less stress remains... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Until your body feels light... clear... at ease... *[pause 4 seconds]*

### Nervous System Reset

Your nervous system is recalibrating now... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Shifting from activation to rest... from fight-or-flight to rest-and-digest... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Your vagus nerve... that calming highway through your body... is activated and strong... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Your heart rate slows to its peaceful baseline... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Your blood pressure normalizes... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Your digestive system resumes its healthy function... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Every system in your body remembers how to be calm... *[pause 4 seconds]*

### Installing Calm as Default

This state of deep relaxation is your natural state... *[pause 2 seconds]*

This is who you are beneath the stress... beneath the demands... *[pause 3 seconds]*

And you can return to this state anytime you choose... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Simply by taking three slow breaths... *[pause 2 seconds]*... and remembering this feeling... *[pause 3 seconds]*

Calm is not something you have to create... it's something you return to... *[pause 4 seconds]*

---

## Integration (1 min)

You've given yourself profound rest today... *[pause 2 seconds]*

Your body has released what it no longer needs... *[pause 2 seconds]*

And remembered its natural state of peace... *[pause 3 seconds]*

This calm stays with you... available in every moment... *[pause 3 seconds]*

---

## Awakening (1 min)

In a moment, I'll count from one to five... and you'll return feeling refreshed, relaxed, and renewed... *[pause 2 seconds]*

One... beginning to return... *[pause 1 second]*

Two... energy flowing back into your body... *[pause 1 second]*

Three... becoming more alert... *[pause 1 second]*

Four... taking a deeper breath... *[pause 1 second]*

Five... eyes open... fully present... deeply relaxed...

*[pause 3 seconds]*

Take your time... Notice how you feel... *[pause 2 seconds]*

## Post-Session Notes

**Practice Frequency**: Daily, especially during high-stress periods

**Best Time**: Evening before sleep, or midday as a reset

**Physiological Effects**:
- Activates parasympathetic nervous system
- Reduces cortisol levels
- Lowers blood pressure and heart rate
- Improves HRV (heart rate variability)

**Progressive Benefits**:
- Week 1: Immediate relaxation during sessions
- Week 2: Faster recovery from stress during the day
- Week 3: Lower baseline stress levels
- Week 4: Improved stress resilience

**3-Breath Anchor**: Three slow breaths anytime → instant calm recall

---

## Clinical Notes

**Mechanism**: Combines progressive relaxation, guided imagery, somatic release, and vagal tone activation to induce deep parasympathetic response.

**Evidence Base**:
- Relaxation Response (Benson) - documented physiological changes
- Polyvagal Theory (Porges) - vagal tone and safety states
- Stress neuroscience - cortisol regulation through relaxation

**Contraindications**: None

**Adaptations**:
- For severe anxiety: Extend induction to 7-8 minutes
- For sleep preparation: Omit awakening (transition to sleep instead)
- For acute stress: Can be shortened to 10 minutes (focus on core work)
