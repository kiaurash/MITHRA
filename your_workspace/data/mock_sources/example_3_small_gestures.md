We often think of intimacy as grand gestures or deep conversations. But research tells a different story: it's the small, consistent moments of connection that build lasting intimacy.

The morning coffee made just the way they like it. The text in the middle of the day saying "thinking of you." The hand reached for during a movie. These micro-moments might seem trivial, but they're deposits in what John Gottman calls the "emotional bank account."

What makes these gestures powerful isn't their size—it's their consistency and intentionality. They say: "I see you. You matter to me. Even in the mundane moments, I'm choosing us."

Think about the small gestures in your own relationship. Which ones light you up when your partner does them? Which ones have you been meaning to do more often but somehow forget?

The beautiful thing about small gestures is that they're accessible. You don't need more time or money or perfect circumstances. You just need to notice and choose.
