## Phase 2: Therapeutic Voice Calibration

**BehaviorShift's therapeutic voice is different from newsletter voice but maintains authenticity:**

### Therapeutic Voice Characteristics:
- **Calm and soothing**: Gentle pacing, reassuring tone
- **Present and grounding**: Uses "you are" rather than "you should be"
- **Permission-giving**: Invites rather than directs
- **Non-prescriptive**: Allows space for individual experience
- **Compassionate**: Warm, accepting, non-judgmental
- **Sensory-rich**: Uses concrete imagery and embodied language
- **Paced for reflection**: Builds in natural pauses

### What to AVOID in Therapeutic Scripts:
- **Commands**: "You must", "You will", "You have to"
- **Urgency**: Fast pacing, pressure, demands
- **Judgment**: "Good/bad", "right/wrong", shame-inducing language
- **Generic affirmations**: Hollow positive statements without grounding
- **Medical claims**: Promises of healing or curing conditions
- **Triggering content**: References to trauma, violence, or distressing scenarios
- **Manipulative language**: Coercive suggestions, boundary violations

### Socratic Elements in Therapeutic Context:
- **Invitation to notice**: "What do you notice in your body right now?"
- **Gentle exploration**: "Perhaps there's a memory or feeling emerging..."
- **Permission for experience**: "Whatever comes up is welcome here"
- **Space for insight**: Pauses for self-discovery
- **Open-ended guidance**: Allows individual interpretation

## Phase 3: Script Structure Design

### Standard Therapeutic Session Structure (15-20 minutes):

**1. Grounding and Permission (2-3 minutes)**
- Invite listener to settle
- Establish safety and consent
- Ground in present moment
- Set intention

**2. Relaxation and Deepening (3-4 minutes)**
- Progressive relaxation (optional)
- Breath awareness
- Deepen relaxation state
- Prepare for insight work

**3. Core Learning Integration (8-10 minutes)**
- Weave in learning objectives from curriculum
- Use stories/metaphors from narrative arc
- Pose reflective questions (with pause)
- Guide toward insights from newsletter
- Connect to user's personal context

**4. Integration and Future Pacing (2-3 minutes)**
- Invite insights to settle
- Imagine applying learning in daily life
- Reinforce empowerment and choice
- Create sense of completion

**5. Return and Closing (1-2 minutes)**
- Gently bring awareness back
- Ground in present
- Express gratitude
- Close session

## Phase 4: SSML Integration for Voice AI

### SSML Tags for Voice Control:

**Pacing and Pauses:**
```xml
<break time="2s"/> <!-- Short pause for reflection -->
<break time="5s"/> <!-- Longer pause for deep reflection -->
<prosody rate="slow">Speak this section more slowly</prosody>
<prosody rate="x-slow">Very slow, meditative pacing</prosody>
```

**Tone and Emphasis:**
```xml
<prosody pitch="-5%">Slightly lower, calming pitch</prosody>
<prosody volume="soft">Quieter, more intimate</prosody>
<emphasis level="moderate">Gentle emphasis on key words</emphasis>
```

**Pronunciation and Breathing:**
```xml
<phoneme alphabet="ipa" ph="bɹiːð">breathe</phoneme> <!-- If needed -->
<say-as interpret-as="spell-out">R-E-L-A-X</say-as> <!-- Rare use -->
```

### Voice Pacing Guidelines:
- **Normal therapeutic pace**: ~120 words per minute
- **Deep relaxation sections**: ~80-100 words per minute
- **Reflection pauses**: 3-5 seconds between questions
- **Transition pauses**: 2 seconds between sections
- **Final grounding**: Return to normal conversational pace

## Phase 5: Write Therapeutic Script

### Writing Guidelines:

**Opening (Grounding and Permission):**
```
[Example - adapt to content]

<prosody rate="slow" pitch="-3%">
Find a comfortable position... where you can be fully present for the next few minutes.
<break time="2s"/>

Perhaps you're sitting... or lying down...
allowing your body to be supported.
<break time="2s"/>

There's no need to do anything special here...
no need to achieve any particular state.
<break time="1s"/>
Whatever you're experiencing right now... is exactly right.
<break time="3s"/>
</prosody>
```

**Core Learning Integration:**
- Weave in key concepts from curriculum naturally
- Use stories from narrative arc as guided imagery
- Pose Socratic questions with generous pauses
- Connect to user's personal context (from Step 0)
- Maintain therapeutic voice (not didactic)

**Example Socratic Element:**
```xml
<prosody rate="slow">
And you might notice...
<break time="2s"/>
what happens when you bring gentle curiosity...
<break time="1s"/>
to [key concept from learning objectives].
<break time="5s"/>

There's no right answer here...
<break time="1s"/>
just an invitation to notice...
<break time="2s"/>
what feels true for you.
<break time="5s"/>
</prosody>
```

**Personalization (Based on User Context):**
- Reference user's specific situation (without naming details)
- Adapt pacing to style preference (practical, reflective, etc.)
- Honor user's learning goals from Step 0
- Use language that fits their context

**Safety-First Writing:**
- Never make promises of healing or outcomes
- Always give permission to stop or disengage
- Avoid triggering scenarios or imagery
- Use inclusive, trauma-informed language
- Respect boundaries and autonomy
