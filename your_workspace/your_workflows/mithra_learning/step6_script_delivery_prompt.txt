**"The audio sounds robotic"**
- Try a different TTS voice

- Or re-generate with adjusted pause durations

**"I fell asleep"**
- Normal! Try sitting up rather than lying down
- Or listen when more alert (morning vs. evening)

**"I don't feel anything happening"**
- Integration happens over time, not immediately
- Continue practice for a week before evaluating
- Focus on whether you can recall concepts more easily
