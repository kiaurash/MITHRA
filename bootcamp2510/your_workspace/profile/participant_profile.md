**What They're Working On:**
An AI-powered behavioral learning platform that addresses the knowing-doing gap in soft skills development. The platform uses a three-dimensional approach: cognitive learning through stories and metaphors, active practice via Socratic coaching and role-play, and subconscious reprogramming through personalized hypnosis and guided visualization. The core insight is that people struggle to change behavior even when they intellectually understand what they should do—as evidenced by doctors who smoke despite knowing the health risks. The product aims to embed new behavioral patterns into users' neurology through repeated, personalized iterations.

**What They're Hoping to Achieve:**
Build a working prototype with core capabilities that can be tested and validated during the bootcamp. Kia wants to move from concept to something tangible that demonstrates the three-dimensional learning approach in action.

**Technical Approach:**

## Project Idea

**Status:** Defined

**Project Title:** BehaviorShift - AI-Powered Behavioral Learning Platform

**Description:**
An AI-powered platform that helps people master soft skills through a three-dimensional approach: cognitive learning via stories and metaphors, active practice through Socratic coaching and role-play, and subconscious reprogramming through personalized hypnosis and guided visualization. The system creates a complete learning experience that embeds new behavioral patterns into users' neurology through repeated, personalized iterations.

**Why This Project:**
Addresses the fundamental knowing-doing gap in behavioral change—people struggle to change their behavior even when they intellectually understand what they should do differently (like doctors who smoke despite knowing the health consequences). This platform bridges that gap by working on multiple levels: cognitive understanding, active practice, and subconscious reprogramming, creating lasting behavioral change rather than just information transfer.
